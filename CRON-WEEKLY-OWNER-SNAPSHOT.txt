ilovepbj — weekly owner snapshot email cron
==========================================

Script: /var/www/html/cli/send-weekly-owner-snapshots.php

Opt-in: restaurant_settings.settings_json.weeklyOwnerSnapshotEmail = true
        (toggle on /admin/reports/weekly-snapshot)

Suggested cron (Monday 7:00 America/Chicago):

  # /etc/cron.d/ilovepbj-weekly-snapshot
  MAILTO=""
  0 7 * * 1 root TZ=America/Chicago /usr/bin/php /var/www/html/cli/send-weekly-owner-snapshots.php >> /var/log/ilovepbj-weekly-snapshot.log 2>&1

Dry-run:
  php /var/www/html/cli/send-weekly-owner-snapshots.php --dry-run

Requires SMTP secrets (same as trial reminders). In-app "Email me this" works when SMTP is configured.
